The company

Bighorn is a Reno construction and hardscape company owned and run by Caleb Sharron. We build the ground around your home or business: concrete, pavers, lawns and fences, and the dirt work underneath all of it.

Services

From the first cut of dirt to the last paver.

Most outdoor projects need dirt work, a solid base, the surface, water and a fence. Bighorn does all of it, so you are not lining up three contractors and hoping they show up in the right order.

How it works

From first call to final walkthrough.

No sales team and no hand-offs. Five steps, the same on a gate repair as on a full backyard.

Our process
  1. 01

    Reach out

    Call, text or email. A few photos of the space and a rough idea of what you want are plenty to start.

  2. 02

    Walkthrough

    Caleb comes out to measure, look at the soil, slope and access, and talk through options and budget with you.

  3. 03

    Free estimate

    You get the scope, materials and price spelled out, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts.

  4. 04

    Build

    Dirt and base first, then the work you will see. You will know the schedule and who to call.

  5. 05

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished job with you, take care of anything that is not right, and leave the site clean.
