The owner · Bighorn Construction & Hardscape

Bighorn Construction & Hardscape is an owner-run company in Reno, Nevada. We build the ground around homes and businesses: concrete walkways and pads, paver patios, sod and sprinkler systems, fences and gates, and the dirt work underneath all of it.

We are licensed, insured and bonded. Estimates are free, and the person you talk to on the phone is the person responsible for your job.
