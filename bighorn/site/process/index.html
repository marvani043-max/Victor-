Our process

From first call to final walkthrough.

The same five steps on a gate repair and on a full backyard. You talk to the owner the whole way through.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Call, text or email. A few photos of the space and a rough idea of what you want are plenty to start.

    Usually a few minutes on the phone.

  2. 02

    Walkthrough

    Caleb comes out to measure, look at the soil, slope and access, and talk through options and budget with you.

    On site, at a time that works for you.

  3. 03

    Free estimate

    You get the scope, materials and price spelled out, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts.

    No cost, no obligation.

  4. 04

    Build

    Dirt and base first, then the work you will see. You will know the schedule and who to call.

    Owner on the job.

  5. 05

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished job with you, take care of anything that is not right, and leave the site clean.

    Done when you are happy with it.

Before the walkthrough

What helps us price it right.

  • Photos of the spaceWide shots from a few angles, plus anything that worries you: cracks, pooling, slopes.
  • Rough measurementsPacing it off is fine. We measure properly on site.
  • What you want to use it forDinners outside, a place for the dog, parking a trailer. It changes the build.
  • A budget range, if you have oneIt lets us suggest the right materials instead of guessing.

Below the surface

Where most of the work goes.

Freeze–thaw cycles in Northern Nevada punish a thin base. Here is what sits under a paver patio built to last. Tap a layer.

Interlocking concrete pavers. Thicker units go under anything with tires.

Swept into every joint and set with water, it locks the field together and keeps weeds and ants out.

A screeded layer the pavers are set into. Too much and they wander; too little and they rock.

Crushed aggregate, compacted in lifts. This is the layer that resists frost heave and settling, and the one cheap jobs skimp on.

On clay or soft soil, fabric keeps the base from pushing down into the dirt below.

Dug out, graded so water runs away from the house, and compacted before anything goes on top.

Spiked along each open edge to hold the field in place, so pavers do not creep apart.

Questions

Before you call.

Don’t see yours? Call or text 775-440-3757.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Call, text or email to set up a walkthrough. The estimate costs nothing and there is no obligation to book.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Bighorn Construction & Hardscape is licensed, insured and bonded in Nevada. You can look up any Nevada contractor on the Nevada State Contractors Board website. License: NV Lic. license # to add. Look up a contractor on NSCB.

What areas do you serve?

Reno, Sparks, Sun Valley, Spanish Springs, Washoe Valley, Carson City, Dayton, Fernley, Silver Springs and Fallon. If you are nearby and not on the list, ask.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. A single walkway, a gate, a shed pad or a sprinkler repair. Tell us what you have.

Do you work for businesses?

Yes. Commercial remodels and demolition, plus concrete and hardscape for commercial properties.

When is the best time to build in Northern Nevada?

Spring through fall is the main season for concrete, pavers and sod. Planning and some dirt work carry on through winter. Booking a walkthrough early gets you a better spot on the schedule.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size, access and weather. Most walkways and patios take days, not months, and the schedule is part of your estimate.
