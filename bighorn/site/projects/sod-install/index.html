The challenge

A backyard of bare dirt inside old brick garden walls, with a concrete walk around the edge.

What we did

The ground was graded and raked smooth, then sod was rolled out in tight, staggered rows right up to the walk and the walls.

The result

A full lawn, edge to edge, ready to water in the same day.
