Backyard Design & Redesign

New-build lots and tired yards that are not working. We lay out where the patio, lawn, planters and paths go, then build all of it with one crew and one schedule.

Get a free estimateCall 775-440-3757

A backyard planned around how you actually use it.

Good backyards start with a few plain questions. Where does the sun sit in the afternoon? Where do the kids or the dog run? Where does water go when it rains? Answer those first and the layout mostly draws itself.

Because Bighorn does the dirt work, concrete, pavers, sprinklers and fencing in house, the plan does not get split across three contractors who each blame the other. One person is accountable from the first stake to the final walkthrough.

How we do it

The order of work

  1. 01

    Walk the yard

    Sun, slope, drainage, access and how you want to use the space.

  2. 02

    Lay it out

    Patio, lawn, beds and paths placed and priced as one plan.

  3. 03

    Build in order

    Dirt and drainage first, then hardscape, irrigation, lawn and finish.

  4. 04

    Walk it together

    A final walkthrough so nothing is left unfinished.

Questions

Common questions

Something else? Call or text 775-440-3757.

Do I need a full design to get started?

No. A few photos and a rough idea are enough for a first conversation. We will sketch the layout with you at the walkthrough.

Can it be built in phases?

Yes. Plenty of yards get the dirt work and hardscape this season and the rest next year. We will plan it so nothing has to be torn out later.
