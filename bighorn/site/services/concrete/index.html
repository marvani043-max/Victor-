Concrete

Walkways, patio slabs, equipment pads and driveways. Dug out, compacted, formed and poured on ground that has been prepared to carry it.

Get a free estimateCall 775-440-3757

Concrete that stays flat and drains away from the house.

Most concrete failures start underneath. Soft fill, no base, and water running back toward the foundation are what crack slabs and lift walkways. So the work starts with the ground: dig out to firm soil, bring in and compact base, and set the forms so water sheds away from the house.

Then it is a clean pour and a clean finish, with control joints where the slab will want to move. Tear-out of the old concrete and haul-off are part of the job when there is something to replace.

What’s included

  • Walkways and side yards
  • Patio slabs
  • Driveways and approaches
  • Shed, AC and equipment pads
  • Steps and landings
  • Tear-out and replacement

How we do it

The order of work

  1. 01

    Dig and compact

    Excavate to firm ground and compact a base under the full footprint.

  2. 02

    Form to drain

    Forms set with a slight fall so water runs off the slab and away from the house.

  3. 03

    Pour and finish

    Placed, screeded and finished, with control joints cut where the slab can crack cleanly.

  4. 04

    Clean up

    Forms pulled, edges backfilled and the site cleaned before we leave.

Typical detail

What goes under a walkway.

Slab
4 in concrete
Base
4 in compacted aggregate
Fall
⅛–¼ in per ft, away from the house
Control joints
8–10 ft apart, cut to ¼ of the slab depth
At the house
An isolation joint, so the slab moves on its own

Typical numbers for Northern Nevada homes. Your soil, slope and use set the final specs, and they are written into your estimate.

Common questions

Something else? Call or text 775-440-3757.

Concrete or pavers?

Concrete usually costs less up front and suits driveways and simple walkways. Pavers cost more but handle ground movement better, and a damaged paver can be replaced on its own. If you are not sure, we can price both.

Can you replace a cracked slab?

Yes. We break out and haul away the old concrete, fix what failed underneath, and pour new.
