Remodels & Construction

Bighorn works indoors and for businesses too: stair renovations, demolition and commercial remodels. Same standard of work as the yards.

Get a free estimateCall 775-440-3757

Remodels and construction, inside and out.

Interior jobs reward the same habits as outdoor ones: careful prep, square layout and a clean finish. A stair rebuild starts with stripping back to a solid structure, then building each tread and riser so they line up.

For businesses, we take on demolition and remodel work with an eye on keeping the space usable and the site clean. Ask about your project. If it is outside what we do, we will say so.

How we do it

The order of work

  1. 01

    Walk the space

    Scope, access, and what has to stay open during the work.

  2. 02

    Demo and prep

    Old finishes out, structure checked, surfaces prepped.

  3. 03

    Build

    New work laid out square and installed in order.

  4. 04

    Finish and clean

    Details finished and the space handed back clean.

Typical detail

The numbers a good stair has to hit.

Rise
7¾ in max
Run
10 in min
Nosing
¾–1¼ in
Handrail
34–38 in above the nosings
Consistency
Steps in a flight within ⅜ in of each other

Typical numbers for Northern Nevada homes. Your soil, slope and use set the final specs, and they are written into your estimate.
