Dirt Work & Excavation

Yard-scale excavation and grading. Leveling, dig-outs for patios and slabs, fixing drainage that runs toward the house, trenching, and hauling off what is left.

Get a free estimateCall 775-440-3757

The dirt work that every good yard sits on.

Water should leave your house, not visit it. A lot of yards in the valley were graded just well enough to pass inspection, then settled. Re-grading so the ground falls away from the foundation fixes more problems than any surface on top of it.

We handle the smaller jobs big excavators do not want: a patio dig-out, a side yard taken down to grade, a trench for sprinklers or drainage, and the haul-off afterward.

What’s included

  • Yard leveling and grading
  • Patio and slab dig-outs
  • Drainage corrections
  • Trenching
  • Dirt and debris haul-off

How we do it

The order of work

  1. 01

    Check the fall

    Look at where water goes now and where it should go.

  2. 02

    Cut and fill

    Dig out high spots, fill and compact low ones.

  3. 03

    Shape to drain

    Final grade sloped away from the house.

  4. 04

    Haul off

    Spoils and debris taken away, not left in a pile.

Typical detail

Water should leave the house.

Fall
At least 6 in within the first 10 ft (IRC R401.3)
Fill
Placed and compacted in lifts
Roof water
Downspouts carried away from the foundation
Swale
Turns water toward the street

Typical numbers for Northern Nevada homes. Your soil, slope and use set the final specs, and they are written into your estimate.

Questions

Common questions

Something else? Call or text 775-440-3757.

Water pools by my foundation. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Most pooling is a grading problem. We re-grade so the ground falls away from the house, and add drainage where grading alone is not enough.

Is this only for bigger projects?

No. Small dirt work is a big part of what we do, and it is often the first step of a patio, lawn or walkway.
