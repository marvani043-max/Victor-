Sod & Sprinkler Systems

Sod installation, new sprinkler systems, drip lines for planting beds and repairs to the system you have. Graded and prepped so the lawn takes, and zoned so you water what needs it.

Get a free estimateCall 775-440-3757

Lawns and sprinklers made for the high desert.

In Northern Nevada, water is the expensive part of a lawn. A system with sensible zones, the right heads for each area and drip on the beds uses less of it and keeps the grass even.

For new sod, the ground gets cleared, graded and prepped before the first roll goes down, with the sprinklers in and tested first. Then the sod is laid tight, seams staggered, and watered in.

What’s included

  • Sod installation
  • New sprinkler systems
  • Drip irrigation for beds
  • Sprinkler repair
  • Timers and zoning
  • Soil prep and grading

How we do it

The order of work

  1. 01

    Clear and grade

    Old turf and debris out, ground graded to drain.

  2. 02

    Sprinklers first

    Lines trenched, heads set and zones tested before any sod goes down.

  3. 03

    Prep the soil

    Raked smooth and firm so the sod roots in evenly.

  4. 04

    Lay and water in

    Seams tight and staggered, then a deep first watering.

Questions

Common questions

Something else? Call or text 775-440-3757.

Can you fix my existing sprinklers?

Yes. Broken heads, leaks, dead zones and timer problems are all routine repairs.

When is the best time to lay sod here?

Spring and early fall are easiest on new sod in Northern Nevada. Summer installs work with closer attention to watering.
