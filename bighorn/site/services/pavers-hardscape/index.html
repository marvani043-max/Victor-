Pavers & Hardscape

Pavers and hardscape, built from the base up.

Paver patios and walkways, block walls and planters, rock borders and steps. The part you walk on is the last few inches of the job.

Get a free estimate Call 775-440-3757

What’s included

  • Paver patios
  • Walkways and side paths
  • Block retaining walls
  • Raised planters
  • Steps and landings
  • River rock and gravel borders
  • Re-levels and repairs

A paver surface is a system. Under the pavers sits a bedding layer, under that a compacted aggregate base, and under that native soil graded so water drains away. Skip a layer or skimp on compaction and the pavers sink, rock or spread apart within a few seasons.

We build the whole stack, set edge restraint on every open side, and finish the joints so the field locks together. Block walls and raised planters get the same treatment: a solid, level base course first.

How we do it

The order of work.

  1. 01

    Lay out and dig

    Mark the footprint and excavate deep enough for the full base.

  2. 02

    Base in lifts

    Aggregate base placed and compacted in layers, not dumped all at once.

  3. 03

    Screed and set

    A level bedding layer, then pavers set to pattern and cut clean at the edges.

  4. 04

    Lock it in

    Edge restraint on open sides and joint sand swept in and set.

Below the surface

The part you never see decides how long it lasts.

Reno winters swing above and below freezing day after day. Water in the ground freezes, expands and lifts anything on a thin or soft base. A patio that lasts is built in layers, and most of the work is underground.

Interlocking concrete pavers. Thicker units go under anything with tires.

Swept into every joint and set with water, it locks the field together and keeps weeds and ants out.

A screeded layer the pavers are set into. Too much and they wander; too little and they rock.

Crushed aggregate, compacted in lifts. This is the layer that resists frost heave and settling, and the one cheap jobs skimp on.

On clay or soft soil, fabric keeps the base from pushing down into the dirt below.

Dug out, graded so water runs away from the house, and compacted before anything goes on top.

Spiked along each open edge to hold the field in place, so pavers do not creep apart.

Questions

Pavers & hardscape, answered.

Something else? Call or text 775-440-3757.

How long do pavers last?

A paver surface on a properly compacted base lasts for decades. Most problems people see come from a thin or soft base, which is why we spend most of the job below the surface.

Do pavers work for driveways?

Yes, with thicker pavers and a deeper base. Tell us what will drive on it and we will spec it to match.
