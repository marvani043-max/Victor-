Our services

Everything from the dirt up

Seven kinds of work, one contractor. Most projects use three or four of them, which is why it helps when the same crew does all of it.

Concrete

Walkways, patio slabs, equipment pads and driveways. Dug out, compacted, formed and poured on ground that has been prepared to carry it.

  • Walkways and side yards
  • Patio slabs
  • Driveways and approaches
  • Shed, AC and equipment pads
  • Steps and landings
  • Tear-out and replacement
Learn moreGet a quote

Pavers & Hardscape

Paver patios and walkways, block walls and planters, rock borders and steps. The part you walk on is the last few inches of the job.

  • Paver patios
  • Walkways and side paths
  • Block retaining walls
  • Raised planters
  • Steps and landings
  • River rock and gravel borders
Learn moreGet a quote

Backyard Design & Redesign

New-build lots and tired yards that are not working. We lay out where the patio, lawn, planters and paths go, then build all of it with one crew and one schedule.

  • Layout and planning
  • New-build backyards
  • Yard redesigns
  • Grading and drainage
  • Patios, paths and lawn
  • Planters and borders

Sod & Sprinkler Systems

Sod installation, new sprinkler systems, drip lines for planting beds and repairs to the system you have. Graded and prepped so the lawn takes, and zoned so you water what needs it.

  • Sod installation
  • New sprinkler systems
  • Drip irrigation for beds
  • Sprinkler repair
  • Timers and zoning
  • Soil prep and grading

Fencing & Gates

Wood privacy fencing, gates, and side-yard closures. New runs, replacements, and repairs to leaning posts and sagging gates.

  • Wood privacy fencing

  • Side-yard closures
  • Fence replacement
  • Post and gate repair

Dirt Work & Excavation

Yard-scale excavation and grading. Leveling, dig-outs for patios and slabs, fixing drainage that runs toward the house, trenching, and hauling off what is left.

  • Yard leveling and grading
  • Patio and slab dig-outs
  • Drainage corrections
  • Trenching
  • Dirt and debris haul-off
