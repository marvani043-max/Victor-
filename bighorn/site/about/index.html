About Bighorn

A young company with an old-school work ethic.

Bighorn Construction & Hardscape is owned and run by Caleb Sharron. When you call, you talk to him. When the work starts, he is the one accountable for it.

The story

In his own words: “I'm a young contractor with a strong work ethic and a lot of hands-on experience in the trade.”

Caleb started Bighorn to do the kind of work he would want done at his own house: concrete that drains, pavers that stay flat, lawns that take, and fences that stay straight. The company covers the whole yard, from dirt work and excavation to concrete, pavers, sprinklers, sod and fencing, plus remodels and commercial work.

Bighorn is licensed, insured and bonded in Nevada (NV Lic. license # to add). Estimates are free.

How we work

Three things we will not cut corners on.

  • Clean work

    Straight lines, tight joints, level surfaces. And a site that is cleaned up every time, not just at the end.

  • Straight answers

    Clear scope and price before work starts. If something is not a fit for us, we will tell you.

  • Pride in the result

    Outdoor spaces people are proud of. That is the goal on every job, small or large.

The name

Why a bighorn.

The desert bighorn sheep is Nevada’s state animal. It lives on the roughest, steepest ground in the state and thrives there. That seemed like the right standard for a company that spends its days in rocky high-desert soil.

Where tough work meets flawless results.
